## Reconciliations (approved decisions applied; nothing silently changed)

- **Edit / Cancel report (6.2) and Reopen (8.3):** the approved evidence policy keeps submitted reports and committed photos immutable, with no unrestricted citizen overwrite or delete. Proposed reconciliation: *Cancel* becomes a request that adds an auditable status (for example "Withdrawn by reporter") through a trusted RPC without deleting evidence; *Edit* of a submitted report becomes "Add information" (append-only note); *Reopen* becomes a status request the assigned official must accept. Each needs a migration and tests; **not implemented, awaiting the owner's confirmation of this wording.**
- **Delete / Edit of a saved (queued) report (7.1, 7.4):** this is a **draft/abandoned-upload** action, not deletion of submitted evidence, and is allowed: it deletes the local queue row and its local photo copies only, after confirmation, never a committed report or object. Needs a "Synced rows cannot be deleted" guard; it also frees the existing purge logic. Not implemented yet.
- **Save as draft (3.7):** account-isolated, lifecycle-safe, local only; unsent and distinct from the queue. Not implemented yet.
- **Delete account (9.4):** conflicts with preserving committed reports. Reconcile as "request account deactivation": sign-out everywhere, remove devices and profile data, keep reports with the owner link detached, performed by trusted administration. Needs the owner's decision.
- **Post anonymously (3.4, 9.4):** the feed hides the name; the server still needs the owner for the report's history and abuse handling. **Backend + policy decision needed** (what officials see).
- **Comments/replies (5.2, 5.3, 8.2):** not in the approved backend decisions or earlier checkpoints. Whether they are required is a product question to check against `MobileComp_GROUP2.docx.pdf` (not yet read in this checkpoint); if required it is a large feature (tables, RLS, moderation, notifications, photos).
- **Mobile-number login/registration/recovery:** needs an SMS authentication provider; the spare gateway phone is an inbound reporting gateway, not an authentication provider. Marked External.

## What this means for the schedule

Roughly a third of the screens are cosmetic restyling of working flows. The larger items are the five-step reporting flow with camera and multi-photo, comments/replies, notification preferences, issue numbers, search/sort, password recovery (blocked on SMTP) and the SMS two-way flow (blocked on the gateway). The multi-photo and comments items change the database contract and must not be started without the two product confirmations above.
